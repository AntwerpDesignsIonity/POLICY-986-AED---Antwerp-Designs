## Online Research & Findings

This project incorporates research from various online sources to inform policy decisions and design choices:
- Industry best practices in AED (Automated External Defibrillator) deployment
- European Union safety standards and regulations
- Ionity charging station network accessibility guidelines
- Public health and safety requirements for AED installations

## Analysis & Key Insights

### Technical Analysis
- Strategic placement of AED devices at charging station locations
- Integration with existing Ionity infrastructure
- Compliance with regional and EU-wide safety regulations

### Impact Assessment
- Enhanced safety for electric vehicle drivers and passengers
- Contribution to public health emergency response capabilities
- Alignment with corporate social responsibility objectives

## Authentic Perspective

This initiative represents a genuine commitment to public safety and well-being. By integrating AED devices into the Ionity charging network, we're not just providing a charging service—we're creating safer spaces for travelers across Europe. This policy reflects our core values of innovation, responsibility, and care for the communities we serve.
